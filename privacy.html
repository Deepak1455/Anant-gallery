DK indus Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Anant Gallery mobile application. This Privacy Policy details our transparent commitment to your privacy, personal data rights, and encryption standards.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Identity: Your email address and display name obtained via Firebase Authentication for account creation, multi-device backup sync, and secure sessions.
  • Photos & Media Files: Only user-selected photos uploaded explicitly for unlimited cloud backup, custom album categorization, and your encrypted private vault.
  • Biometric Security: Biometric fingerprint and PIN authentication operate 100% locally on your device via standard Android WebAuthn platform APIs. We never transmit or store your fingerprint biometric records on our servers.

2. Data Security & Encryption

Your photos, database records, and sync queues are encrypted end-to-end in transit using modern HTTPS/TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols to prevent unauthorized access.

3. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

Complete Control Over Your Memories: You have the absolute right to delete your data at any time.

In-App Instant Deletion: Open the app ➔ Go to My Profile ➔ Danger Zone ➔ Delete My Account & Data to permanently wipe all your photos, albums, and user credentials immediately.
