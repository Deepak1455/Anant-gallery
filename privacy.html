Privacy Policy for Anant Gallery

Last Updated: March 2025

DK indus Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Anant Gallery mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: Email address and profile name for account creation and authentication via Firebase.
  • Photos & Media: User-selected photos uploaded solely for cloud backup, album organization, and vault security features.
  • Biometric Data: Local device hardware authentication (Fingerprint/PIN) processed entirely on your device via standard WebAuthn APIs. We never transmit or store your fingerprint biometric data on our servers.

2. Data Security & Storage

All data and photos are encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. Cloud backups are stored securely.

3. Data Deletion & User Rights

Users have full control over their data. You can permanently delete individual photos, clear albums, or permanently delete your entire account and all cloud-stored photos directly from the Profile -> Danger Zone section within the app at any time.
