Privacy Policy - Anant Gallery

Official Policy & Data Rights

Last Updated: March 2025

DK indus Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Anant Gallery mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address and profile display name collected via Firebase Authentication solely for user account sync and secure session management.
  • Photos & Media Files: User-selected photos uploaded solely for personal cloud backup, album organization, and secure private vault storage.
  • Biometric Data: Device fingerprint authentication is processed 100% locally on your device via standard Android WebAuthn platform APIs. We never store or transmit your biometric records.

2. Data Security & Encryption

All photos, login tokens, and communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

3. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

Full Control Over Your Data: You can permanently delete your entire account, custom albums, and all cloud-stored photos at any time directly inside the app under My Profile ➔ Danger Zone ➔ Delete My Account & Data.

You can also request complete manual data deletion by emailing our support desk at:
