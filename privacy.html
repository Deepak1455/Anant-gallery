Deepak Thakur ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Anant Gallery mobile application (Package Name: com.anantgallery.anantgallery). This Privacy Policy details our transparent commitment to your privacy, personal data rights, and highest security standards.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Identity: Your email address, display name, and unique user ID via Firebase Authentication for account creation, multi-device backup sync, and secure sessions.
  • Photos & Media Files: Access to photos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) is requested strictly when you choose to backup, organize into albums, or encrypt photos in your private vault. We never sell, scan, or share your personal photos.
  • Biometric Security: Biometric fingerprint and PIN authentication operate 100% locally on your device via standard Android BiometricPrompt APIs. We never transmit or store your biometric records on external servers.

2. Data Security & Encryption

Your photos, database records, and sync queues are encrypted end-to-end in transit using modern HTTPS/TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols to prevent unauthorized access.

3. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

Complete Control Over Your Memories: You have the absolute right to delete your data at any time.

In-App Instant Deletion: Open the app → Go to My Profile & Settings → Danger Zone → Delete My Account & All Data to permanently wipe all your photos, albums, and user credentials immediately.

Manual Request: You can also email us directly at anantgalleryogr@gmail.com, and we will wipe your cloud data within 48 hours.
